Method & assumptions

  • Wet concrete volume is converted to dry volume with the standard factor 1.54.
  • Cement density 1,440 kg/m³, one bag = 50 kg. Sand and aggregate are shown in m³ and ft³.
  • Cement is rounded UP to whole bags for purchase; cost uses whole bags and the displayed (2-decimal) sand/aggregate ft³.
  • Mix ratios are nominal mixes (M10 1:3:6, M15 1:2:4, M20 1:1.5:3, M25 1:1:2). Confirm the mix with your structural engineer.
  • Prices are your own inputs, not market rates. This tool estimates quantities only; it is not a structural design.
